What are the correct Instagram image dimensions?

Instagram supports several content formats, and each one has a recommended pixel size. Using the correct dimensions ensures your images display at full resolution without compression artifacts or unexpected cropping. Here are the key sizes you need to know:

  • Instagram Post (square): 1080 × 1080 pixels, 1:1 aspect ratio. The classic square format that dominates the feed grid.
  • Instagram Post (portrait): 1080 × 1350 pixels, 4:5 aspect ratio. Takes up more vertical space in the feed, which can improve engagement.
  • Instagram Story: 1080 × 1920 pixels, 9:16 aspect ratio. Full-screen vertical format designed for Stories.
  • Instagram Reel cover: 1080 × 1920 pixels, 9:16 aspect ratio. The thumbnail that appears in the Reels tab and on your profile grid.
  • Instagram Profile picture: 320 × 320 pixels, 1:1 aspect ratio. Displayed as a circle, but uploaded as a square.
  • Instagram Carousel: 1080 × 1080 or 1080 × 1350 pixels. All slides in a carousel must share the same aspect ratio, set by the first image.

If your source image doesn't match these dimensions, you'll need to crop it before uploading. That's exactly what freecropimage.app is built for.

What image quality is best for Instagram?

Instagram compresses every image you upload, so starting with the highest quality source gives the best final result. Here are some practical tips:

  • Resolution: Always crop to at least 1080 pixels on the shortest side. Instagram downscales images wider than 1080 pixels anyway, so there's no benefit to uploading at 4K.
  • Format: JPG is the standard choice for photographs. Use 80–90% quality for a good balance between file size and sharpness. PNG is better for graphics, logos, and images with text, since it preserves hard edges without compression artifacts.
  • Color space: Stick with sRGB. Instagram converts other color profiles to sRGB on upload, and the conversion can shift colors unexpectedly.
  • File size: Keep images under 8 MB. Larger files trigger heavier server-side compression.

In freecropimage.app, you can choose your output format (JPG, PNG, or WebP) and adjust the quality slider before downloading. This lets you control the trade-off between file size and image sharpness.

Why does square cropping matter for Instagram?

Your Instagram profile grid displays every post as a square thumbnail, regardless of the original aspect ratio. If you upload a landscape or portrait image without planning for the square crop, Instagram's auto-crop may center on the wrong part of the image, cutting off faces, text, or key visual elements.

By cropping to a square (or at least previewing the square center crop) before you post, you maintain visual consistency across your entire grid. A cohesive grid signals professionalism and attention to detail, which matters for brands, photographers, and creators building an audience. Instagram's algorithm also tends to favor content that looks polished and generates engagement, so well-cropped images contribute to better reach over time.

For carousel posts, consistency is even more important. Every slide inherits the aspect ratio of the first image. If you mix aspect ratios by accident, some slides will have unexpected letterboxing. Cropping all slides to the same dimensions beforehand with freecropimage.app avoids this entirely.
