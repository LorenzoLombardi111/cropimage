What are the correct YouTube image dimensions?

YouTube uses specific pixel sizes for different image types across the platform. Using the correct dimensions ensures your images display sharply without letterboxing, stretching, or unexpected cropping. Here are the three sizes you need to know:

  • YouTube Thumbnail: 1280 × 720 pixels, 16:9 aspect ratio. This is the recommended resolution for custom thumbnails. The minimum width is 640 pixels, but 1280 × 720 delivers the best clarity across all devices. The file must be under 2 MB.
  • YouTube Channel Banner: 2560 × 1440 pixels. This is the full upload size, but the visible safe area varies by device. Desktop shows roughly 2560 × 423 pixels in the center, while mobile crops tighter. Designing at the full 2560 × 1440 and keeping key content centered ensures it looks correct everywhere.
  • YouTube Channel Icon: 800 × 800 pixels, 1:1 aspect ratio. Displayed as a circle on the channel page and next to every comment you leave. Upload a square image and YouTube will apply the circular mask automatically.

What makes a great YouTube thumbnail?

Beyond getting the dimensions right, effective thumbnails share several visual characteristics that make them stand out in a crowded feed:

  • High contrast: Thumbnails appear at small sizes in search results and sidebars. Bold contrast between foreground and background ensures your image reads clearly even at 160 × 90 pixels.
  • Faces and expressions: Human faces with strong expressions draw the eye and create an emotional connection. Close-up crops of faces tend to outperform wide shots.
  • Readable text at small sizes: If you add text to your thumbnail, use large, bold fonts with high contrast against the background. Limit yourself to three or four words. If the text is not legible at thumbnail size, it is not helping.
  • Bright, saturated colors: Vivid colors stand out against YouTube's white and dark interface. Avoid muted tones that blend into the background of the page.

Crop your source image to emphasize these qualities. A tight crop that fills the frame with your subject almost always outperforms a wide shot with lots of empty space.

What quality and format settings work best for YouTube?

YouTube accepts JPG, PNG, GIF, and WebP thumbnails, but not every format is equally suited for the job:

  • JPG at 85–95% quality is the best choice for most thumbnails, especially those based on photographs. This quality range produces sharp, vibrant images while keeping the file size well under YouTube's 2 MB limit.
  • PNG works well for thumbnails with bold graphic elements, text overlays, or logos where you need crisp edges without compression artifacts. PNG files tend to be larger, so check that your export stays under the 2 MB ceiling.
  • WebP is a modern alternative that offers smaller file sizes at equivalent quality. YouTube accepts WebP, and freecropimage.app supports it as an export option.

Avoid going below 80% JPG quality. Compression artifacts become visible around text and high-contrast edges, which are exactly the elements that make thumbnails effective. In freecropimage.app, you can adjust the quality slider and preview the result before downloading.
