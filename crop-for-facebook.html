What are the correct Facebook image dimensions?

Facebook uses specific pixel dimensions for each placement. Getting them right ensures your images look sharp on both desktop and mobile feeds. Here are the sizes you need to know:

  • Cover Photo: 820 × 312 pixels (displays at 640 × 360 on mobile)
  • Profile Picture: 170 × 170 pixels (128 × 128 on mobile)
  • Post / Link Share Image: 1200 × 630 pixels
  • Event Cover Image: 1920 × 1005 pixels
  • Group Cover Photo: 1640 × 856 pixels

These are the current recommended sizes as of 2026. Facebook occasionally adjusts its layout, but these dimensions have remained stable across the most recent updates. When in doubt, use the highest resolution listed—Facebook will scale down gracefully.

What are the text-safe zones on Facebook covers?

The Facebook cover photo displays differently depending on the device. On desktop, the full 820 × 312 area is visible. On mobile, the image is cropped to a taller, narrower region centered on the original. This means the left and right edges of your cover photo may be hidden on phones.

To keep important elements visible everywhere, follow these guidelines:

  • Keep text, logos, and key visuals within the center 640 pixels of width.
  • Avoid placing anything critical in the bottom-left corner—your profile picture overlaps that area on desktop.
  • Use freecropimage.app to preview how the crop looks before you upload, so you can reposition if needed.
